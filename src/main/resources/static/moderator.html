Moderation Guidelines
Review a resource for:
  • Academic relevance and usefulness to students.
  • Readable, complete and correctly formatted content.
  • Correct subject and branch information.
  • Potential plagiarism or copyright concerns.
  • Incorrect, misleading or outdated information.
Before making a moderation decision:
  • Open and inspect the uploaded PDF.
  • Check that its content matches its title and subject.
  • Do not approve material that you have not reviewed.
  • Use backend moderation records once that feature is implemented.
